> Oh Lord, here I am,\
> in the midst of this crowd.\
> Am I showing off?\
> Am I being fake?\
> Am I being led by fear?\
> Am I filled with terror?\
> What shall I do, Lord?
>
> Yes, Lord, this unknown fear creeps in.\
> I don't know where it comes from.\
> What shall I do, Lord?\
> I don't know.\
> If I had known, I would have done...\
> I don't know what sin I have committed,\
> what detestable things I am holding onto.
>
> Lord, I turn to you.\
> For you are the one who is holding me.\
> You are my love.\
> You are my life.\
> You are my heart.\
> I lean on you.\
> O God, do not leave me alone.
>
> My God is faithful.\
> He is right here,\
> comforting me,\
> leading me,\
> strengthening me,\
> filling me with love.
>
> I turn to you, Lord.\
> I give all to you, Lord,\
> whether I am feeling it or not.\
> I pronounce it again and again: the Lord is my King.
>
> I was dead, but you gave me this life.\
> I hold your hand, because you were holding me before I was born.\
> Thank you, my God.\
> Here I am.
